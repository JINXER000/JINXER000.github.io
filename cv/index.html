Research Projects

  • 2024 - now
    Demonstration Skill Decomposition and Online Reorganization (ITF Research Talent Hub), Project Lead
    • Overview: Designed the TAMP-gated multi-skill imitation learning framework; developed algorithms for skill decomposition, geometric safety checking, and online replanning; led real-world experimental validations on dual-arm robotic hardware.
    • Result: Decomposed expert demonstrations into reusable primitives for online task reorganization, reaching over 4x the success rate of the mainstream pi-0.5 baseline in few-shot and out-of-distribution settings; conditionally accepted by IEEE T-RO and released as open source.
    Homepage / Code
  • 2024 - 2025
    Fine Bimanual Manipulation for Flexible Assembly (Huawei), Co-Lead
    • Overview: Designed a VLM-based task understanding module for bimanual coordination mode recognition and keypose classification; introduced a consistency model to reduce policy inference latency; assisted in deploying and validating the algorithms on dual-arm Franka robot setups.
    • Result: Deployed on dual-arm Franka platforms and accepted by IEEE T-ASE (Funding: RMB 1,000,000).
    Code
  • Intelligent Navigation and Robust Flight Control for Unmanned Systems (Hong Kong RGC-GRF), Participant / Direction Lead
    • Overview: Formulated and implemented CUDA-accelerated incremental ESDF algorithms; built the GPU parallel mapping and online planning pipeline; conducted deployment and flight/driving tests on UGV and UAV hardware platforms.
    • Result: Achieved a 3x speedup in distance field updating and a 10x improvement in environment representation precision; released publicly one year ahead of NVIDIA nvblox with comparable mapping speed and higher distance-field precision; published in RA-L 2022 and presented at RCAR 2021 and IROS 2022.
    Code
  • Advanced Motion Planning for Multi-Agent Systems Coordination (Hong Kong RGC-GRF), Participant / Direction Lead
    • Overview: Developed sampling-based task and motion planning algorithms under linear temporal logic (LTL) constraints for multi-stage tasks with strict temporal and safety requirements; built an interactive multi-task system supporting online goal and constraint adjustments; validated on mobile robots, quadrotors, and aerial manipulators.
    • Result: Cut replanning reaction time by 5x; published at ICRA 2023, IROS 2023, and ROBIO 2024 (Best Paper in Automation Finalist), and filed as a Chinese invention patent.
    Code
